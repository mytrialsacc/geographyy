🎙️ Spoken Script:

"Four entire European countries fit inside this one—and almost nobody lives in it. Mongolia is the size of Western Europe, but has fewer people than Madrid. That's barely 2 people per square kilometre—the lowest on Earth. Less than 1% of the land can grow crops due to high altitude and harsh soil. So for centuries, survival meant moving herds across hundreds of miles. Today, half the country has moved into a single city valley. Making the emptiest country on Earth also one of the most crowded. Would you live in a country with this much empty space? Tell me below."
