The Scale Hook · Itna Bada Khaali Mulk

0:00 – 0:45 105 words
🎙️ Poori Voiceover Narration (BEAT 01):

Four entire European countries—the United Kingdom, France, Germany, and Italy—could fit inside this territory. Yet the total population of this vast land is smaller than the single city of Madrid. Mongolia spans one point five six million square kilometres, but across this entire expanse live just three point four million people. That produces an average density of barely two people per square kilometre—the lowest on Earth. But the real geographic mystery isn't just that Mongolia is empty. It is that half of the entire country's population now lives crammed inside a single valley.

Ger Districts · Shehar Me Dhuan Phansna

3:00 – 4:15 165 words
🎙️ Poori Voiceover Narration (BEAT 04):

When herders arrive in Ulaanbaatar, they claim free plots of land on the steep hillsides encircling the capital. Today, these spontaneous settlements—known as the Ger Districts—house over eight hundred thousand people, more than half the city's population. In winter, temperatures plunge below minus thirty degrees, making Ulaanbaatar the coldest capital on Earth. Because these districts lack central heating, families burn raw coal in simple stoves inside their yurts. The city sits trapped at the bottom of a bowl-shaped valley. Cold air pools at the floor, creating a severe thermal inversion that traps dense black smoke beneath a ceiling of warm air, spiking pollution to fifty times safe limits.
