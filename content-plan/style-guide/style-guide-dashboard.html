Thumbnail 3-Element Rule (§27)

Non-Negotiable
  • 1
    Subject Map / Landmass: Clean, high-contrast silhouette with subtle topo relief.
  • 2
    Scale Anchor / Comparison: Inset shape, measurement ring, or split line creating immediate visual curiosity.
  • 3
    3–5 Word Text Sticker: High-impact uppercase sticker font with dark solid shadow (e.g., "14 TIMES BIGGER").

Audio & Voice Direction (§22–§23)

Broadcast Spec
Loudness Normalization: -14 LUFS integrated (YouTube Standard), Peak at -1.0 dBFS max.
Narrator Cadence: 135 to 145 words per minute. Calm, authoritative, analytical tone. No exaggerated YouTube hype.
Music & Ducking: Music ducks automatically to -18 dB to -22 dB under dialogue. Low-frequency cinematic drones and subtle ticks.
