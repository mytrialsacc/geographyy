Maryam · sab se pehle yeh parho

Is site se video kaise banani hai?

Maryam, is site ko upar se neeche follow karna hai. Tumhein script likhni, English samajhni, Flow prompt banana ya timing calculate nahi karni. Har jagah jo button aur file naam diya hai, bas wahi use karna hai.

Sab se zaroori baat: yeh site khud video nahi banati aur tumhari files save nahi karti. Yeh tayyar script, Google Flow prompts, cut timings aur TikTok upload text deti hai. Awaaz F5-TTS mein, clips Google Flow mein aur final video CapCut mein banti hai.

04

Long video ka poora order

  1. Calendar kholo aur jis week par kaam karna hai woh dekho.
  2. Lambi videos kholo aur us week ka Long studio kholo.
  3. 01 Voiceover: “Poora script copy karo” dabao. F5-TTS kholo, script paste karo, voice bm_mix_adam_lewis aur speed 0.8 select karke audio banao. Download ko page par diya exact naam do.
  4. Wapas studio page par aao. 02 Voice upload mein wahi MP3 select karo. Neeche ke clip timings asli voice ke mutabiq update ho jayenge.
  5. 03 Google Flow: Google Flow kholo. New project mein model Video → Veo 3.1 Lite, ratio 16:9, duration 8 seconds, output 1.
  6. Clip 01 ka prompt copy karke Flow mein paste aur Generate. Sahi result download karke card par diya exact filename rakho. Isi tarah clip 40 tak.
  7. Map/coastline ghalat, extra text, logo, broken object ya ajeeb movement ho to woh clip accept mat karo; dobara Generate.
  8. 04 CapCut: naya 16:9 project. Voice Audio Track 1 par zero se. Saare Flow clips filename order mein import aur har clip ka Volume 0.
  9. Page par updated timestamp aur quoted voice line ko dekh kar clip badlo. Gap aaye to halka slow ya clean freeze frame; black screen nahi.
  10. Export 1080p, 30fps aur Long/Final folder mein save.

Yaad: voice upload karne ka matlab website par awaaz bhejna nahi. Browser file ki duration parhta hai aur sirf tumhare phone par timings update karta hai.

05

Teen Shorts ka poora order

  1. Choti videos kholo aur Week 01 ka Short 01 kholo.
  2. Script copy karke F5-TTS mein bm_mix_adam_lewis, speed 0.8 se voice banao. Page ka exact MP3 filename rakho.
  3. Voice Short page par upload karo taake timing update ho.
  4. Google Flow mein model Veo 3.1 Lite, ratio 9:16, duration 8 seconds aur output 1.
  5. Har prompt alag Generate karo. Card ka exact filename rakho aur QC note se clip check karo.
  6. CapCut 9:16 project mein voice zero se. Flow clips ka Volume 0 aur page ke updated timings/cut cues par clips badlo.
  7. Export 1080p, 30fps aur Short 01 folder mein save.
  8. Bilkul isi order se Short 02 aur Short 03 banao.

Ek week tab production-complete hai jab 1 Long aur 3 Shorts export ho chuki hon.

06

Music aur captions

  1. Voice sab se clear rehni chahiye.
  2. Anas approved music de to CapCut mein 5% volume se shuru karo; voice dab rahi ho to aur kam.
  3. Random copyrighted song mat lagao.
  4. English auto captions banao, har spelling check karo aur captions ko center safe area mein rakho.
  5. TikTok buttons ke neeche ya YouTube edge ke paas text mat rakho.
07

YouTube aur TikTok par upload

  1. YouTube Long ke liye Long studio page ka title bilkul same rakho. Category Education, language English aur audience Not made for kids.
  2. YouTube Short ke liye us Short page ka title same rakho. Original 9:16 file upload karo, kisi doosre platform ke watermark wali copy nahi.
  3. Upload pehle Private rakho. Title, captions, cover, audio aur ratio check karke Schedule/Public.
  4. TikTok ke liye Week 01 TikTok upload packs kholo.
  5. Short 01 ke liye sirf Short 01 card ka caption, cover text aur filename. Isi tarah Short 02 aur Short 03.
  6. Extra #fyp ya random hashtags mat lagao.
08

In alfaaz ka matlab

Script

Woh English narration jo F5-TTS ko deni hai.

Prompt

English instruction jo Google Flow ko ek clip banane ke liye deni hai.

Clip

Flow se download hone wali 8-second choti video file.

Cut cue

Voice ki quoted line. Yeh line aaye to agla clip lagana hai.

Timestamp

CapCut timeline ka waqt, jaise 0:47.

Volume 0

Flow clip ki apni awaaz band. Sirf F5 voice aur low music sunai den.

16:9

YouTube Long ki horizontal video.

9:16

YouTube Short aur TikTok ki vertical video.

Cover

Post kholne se pehle nazar aane wali image/frame.

Captions

Video par voice ke English words.
