YouTube aur TikTok setup

Maryam: pehle YouTube ka 01–04 setup karo, phir TikTok ka 05–07. Har English box seedha copy karke usi jagah paste karo.

Algorithm ki asal baat: koi secret switch reach nahi deta. YouTube viewer ka click, watch aur satisfaction dekhta hai. TikTok watch behavior aur interactions ke saath caption, sound aur hashtags se topic samajhta hai. Yeh setup channel ko ek saaf geography niche aur consistent identity deta hai.

01

  1. YouTube Studio kholo.
  2. Customization → Profile kholo.
  3. Name aur pehla handle paste karo. Handle unavailable ho to sirf fallback try karo.

YouTube profile aur banner

Profile square banao. Banner 2560 × 1440 px banao aur saara zaroori text bilkul beech ke safe area mein rakho.

A minimal square channel emblem for a visual geography documentary brand, a simple globe crossed by one clean compass needle, deep ocean blue background, warm sand and copper land accents, crisp editorial vector style, strong silhouette readable at tiny size, centered composition, no letters, no words, no numbers, no gradients, no watermark, 1:1

A wide cinematic geography documentary channel banner, a physical globe, an unlabeled relief map and a brass compass arranged on a dark cartography table, soft sunrise light crossing the globe, deep ocean blue, warm sand and copper palette, clean editorial composition, all important objects inside the center safe area, dark edges for responsive cropping, no readable text, no logo, no watermark, 16:9

03

YouTube channel settings

  1. Studio → Settings → Channel → Basic info.
  2. Country of residence mein apna asal country select karo.
  3. Keywords paste karo.
  4. Advanced settings → Audience: No, set this channel as not made for kids.
  5. Feature eligibility: phone/identity verification jo available ho complete karo.

04

YouTube upload defaults

Studio → Settings → Upload defaults. Browser ke bahar upload karo to yeh settings manually check karni hain.

  • Visibility: Private; title, description, thumbnail aur captions check karke Schedule/Public.
  • Title aur description: default khali; har video ka apna verified pack use hoga.
  • Category: Education. Language: English.
  • License: Standard YouTube License.
  • Comments: On; potentially inappropriate comments review ke liye hold.
  • Captions: English captions correct karke publish.
  • Altered content: realistic synthetic scenes hon to upload par honestly disclose.
  • Third-party AI training: off jab tak Saif khud permission na den.

Home tab par Uploads, Shorts aur phir Hidden Geography | Maps Explained playlist lagao.

06

TikTok privacy aur upload settings

  • Account: Public Personal Account.
  • Comments: Everyone; filters on.
  • Duet aur Stitch: On.
  • Downloads: Off, clean copies ki reposting kam karne ke liye.
  • Video: original 1080 × 1920, 9:16, kisi platform ke watermark ke baghair.
  • Captions: English auto captions post se pehle correct karo.
  • Cover: upload pack ka text center safe area mein.
  • Disclosure: realistic AI visuals ka label option aaye to honestly select karo.
07

Har upload ke baad algorithm check

  1. Upload pack ka exact caption use karo; random #fyp aur unrelated hashtags mat lagao.
  2. Main search phrase voice, captions aur post caption mein naturally match honi chahiye.
  3. YouTube par pehle 24 ghantay Home/Suggested CTR aur audience retention dekho.
  4. TikTok par average watch time, full-video watches, search traffic, shares aur saves dekho.
  5. Weak post foran delete/re-upload mat karo. Agli Short ka opening aur cover data se improve karo.
  6. Kisi ek video ka fail hona poore channel ko algorithm penalty nahi deta.
