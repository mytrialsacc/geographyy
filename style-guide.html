Google Flow style guide

Har week ka visual look isi rule par lock rahega.

01

Long settings

  • Video → Veo 3.1 Lite, horizontal 16:9, output 1, har raw clip 8 seconds.
  • Cinematic geography documentary, accurate coastlines, photorealistic terrain aur natural light.
  • Ek continuous shot; slow steady drone, dolly, overhead ya orbital motion.
  • Prompt mein no text, logo, watermark, spoken dialogue aur narration locked hai.
02

Short settings

  • Video → Veo 3.1 Lite, vertical 9:16, output 1, subject center safe area mein.
  • Text Google Flow ke andar generate nahi karna; captions CapCut mein.
  • Map/coastline ya object ghalat ho to clip accept nahi karni—regenerate karo.
  • Har clip ka exact filename page par diya gaya hai.
03

Voice + edit

  • Voiceover F5-TTS se separate generate karo.
  • Voice upload karke page ke timestamps rescale karo.
  • Flow ki saari clip audio CapCut mein Volume 0 karo.
  • Music 5–7% se shuru karo; voice clear rehni chahiye.
  • Long 1080p 16:9 aur Shorts 1080p 9:16, 30fps.
