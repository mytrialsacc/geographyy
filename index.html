Standard Operating Procedure

End-to-End Production Runbook

The deterministic 7-step checklist for publishing every video episode.

  1. Step 1: Fact Ingestion — Gather facts, geographical coordinates, elevation records, and historical datasets into research-sources.txt.
  2. Step 2: Master Scripting — Write long-form documentary script in long-videos-master.md calculated at exactly 150 words/minute (~1,100 words for 7.5 min).
  3. Step 3: 3x Shorts Extraction — Derive Short A (Paradox), Short B (Metric Shock), and Short C (Future Shift) in shorts-master.md (110–135 words each).
  4. Step 4: Voiceover Audio Synthesis — Generate TTS voiceover in ElevenLabs or natural voice generator; verify total duration matches pacing.
  5. Step 5: Visual Cue Sheet Construction — Map spoken words to clip cuts with exact "Switch immediately after" anchors in part-X-voiceover-cue-sheet.md.
  6. Step 6: AI Batch Video Generation — Execute prompts from part-X-flow-prompts.md in Google Flow / Veo / Runway; save as p1-s01.mp4, p1-s02-a.mp4, etc.
  7. Step 7: NLE Timeline Assembly & Publishing — Snap clips to auto-captioned words in CapCut / Premiere with zero guesswork; toggle stages to complete in the web dashboard!
